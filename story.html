Our Story

The story behind the closet

This closet was built over more than ten years. Pieces came from Bangladesh, from Nepal and from Thailand, and over the last six years from Canada too.

Each one was worn, loved and carried from place to place. Stories I Wore is how they find their next owner, so they can start new stories with you.

Stories I Wore began as A Shundori's Closet, selling through social media and Facebook Marketplace. This site makes it easier: every size, measurement, condition and price is right on the page, so you don't need to message to ask.
